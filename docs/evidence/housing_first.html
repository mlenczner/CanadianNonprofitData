What it is

Immediate permanent housing without sobriety/treatment preconditions, plus wraparound supports; housing as the foundation for stability rather than the reward for it.

At Home/Chez Soi (Mental Health Commission of Canada RCT, 5 cities, n=2,148)

Substantially improved housing stability across all five cities and both program types vs. usual care; for the highest-need top 10% of users, every $10 invested returned >$21 in public-cost offsets. World's largest Housing First trial.

Will it work here?

Evidence summarized above comes from specific places, populations, and delivery conditions -- not from Canada in general. Effects measured in one system (funding model, staffing ratios, target population) often shrink, disappear, or reverse when the same program moves to a different one. A registry rating or systematic review answers “does this work somewhere, under study conditions” -- not “will this work here, delivered by this organization, for this population.” Treat every rating above as a starting point for local evaluation, not a guarantee.
